## Fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts via `next/font` (self-hosted at build
time, no third-party runtime requests). All are licensed under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1 (free for commercial use, no attribution required in the
product UI):

- Manrope — interface typeface

- DM Sans, DM Serif Display, DM Serif Text — certificate typefaces
- Mrs Saint Delafield — certificate signature script

## Avatar artwork

Profile avatars are generated locally with [DiceBear](https://www.dicebear.com/)
(the MIT-licensed `@dicebear/core`, with style definitions from the
`@dicebear/styles` package). No third-party request is made at runtime; the SVG
is produced on our own server.

We render the **clay** style, created by DiceBear
(<https://www.dicebear.com/styles/clay/>). Its metadata states the licence as
**CC0 1.0** (public domain,
<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>), so no attribution is
required; it is recorded here for completeness.

## AI-generated content

Assessment questions are generated by an OpenAI-compatible API from
transcripts of the public videos the user selects. Under the provider's
terms, output belongs to us; we claim no ownership over the underlying
video content.
